Models & complexity

Topics 3–5 of the proposed website outline. Full explanations, examples, and references will be added as the project develops.

  1. Quantum Turing machines

    This section will describe the quantum Turing machine model and relate its configurations and transitions to the classical model from class. It will explain how amplitudes, valid quantum evolution, and acceptance probabilities enter the definition of a computation.

  2. Model equivalence & BQP

    This section will outline how quantum Turing machines and uniform quantum circuit families simulate one another with polynomial overhead, including the assumptions needed for this equivalence. It will then define bounded-error quantum polynomial time (BQP) and explain the role of the error bound and amplification.

  3. BQP in the complexity landscape

    This section will compare BQP with the complexity classes covered in the course, using P, BPP, NP, and PSPACE as starting points. A diagram and accompanying discussion will distinguish proved containments from conjectures and unresolved relationships.
